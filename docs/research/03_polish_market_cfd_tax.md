# Polish regulatory, fiscal and market context for a 300 MW BWRX-300 SMR at Stalowa Wola vs gas alternatives

Compiled 20 Sept 2026 from web sources (WebSearch/WebFetch; TGE monthly PDF reports parsed directly). Every figure carries a URL and year. Confidence: **H** = official/primary source read directly; **M** = reputable secondary source or single primary source; **L** = single press quote, memory-flagged or inferred. "NOT FOUND" is stated explicitly where the search did not produce a verifiable number. Nothing below is invented; where I compute something from statutory parameters it is labelled "calc".

Conventions: PLN/MWh unless stated; EUR/PLN 4.36 (NBP, 18 Sept 2026).

## Recommended central assumptions for the model (PLN, 2026 money)

1. **Wholesale price path (day-ahead, baseload capture for a must-run nuclear unit)**
   - 2025 actual: 446 PLN/MWh (TGE). 2026 YTD (Jan–Aug) ≈500; 2026 full-year likely 520–560 given Q4 forward ≈700.
   - Forward-implied: 2027 ≈500 (Aug avg; ≈590 mid-Sept), 2028 ≈445, 2029 ≈420.
   - Central long-run (2030–2045) for a **non-CfD merchant case**: 380–450 PLN/MWh real-2026 baseload, with nuclear capture factor 0.90–0.95 of baseload (solar-driven midday troughs, ≈300–450 negative hours/yr rising) → capture price ≈350–420. This is a judgement bridging TGE 2029 (420) and PEJ counterfactual pricing (strike 470–550 vs "without aid" 780–860 implies the state expects market prices well below 470). **No official Polish 2030/2040 curve was retrieved — flag as key gap.**
   - High case: sustained gas 50+ EUR/MWh & EUA 90+: 550–650 PLN/MWh (2026-type year). Low case: 300–350 with rapid RES+storage.
2. **CfD (if granted, PEJ-template):** strike 125–145 EUR/MWh ≈ **545–630 PLN/MWh** at 4.36 (OSGE ask); fleet-average ambition ≈120 EUR ≈ 525 PLN. PEJ approved band 470–550 PLN/MWh (real). Central: **560 PLN/MWh (2026 real)**, CPI-indexed, **40-year** tenor (PEJ precedent; 60 requested), two-way, monthly settlement, reference price = daily blended TGE index (TGeBase for spot leg), no explicit negative-hour exclusion but no-sale-below-variable-cost rule → treat negative hours as zero output & zero CfD payment. Assume ≥70% exchange sales obligation and annual load-factor/revenue clawback. Probability-weight the CfD (Ministry non-committal; EC notification not started as of July 2026).
3. **Capacity market:** for COD ≤2030 use 465 PLN/kW/yr × KWD 0.938; for 2031+ include only as scenario (successor mechanism not legislated): 0 in base case, 250–450 PLN/kW/yr (CPI-indexed, 17-y) in upside.
4. **Tax:** CIT 19%; tax depreciation: buildings 2.5%, structures 4.5%, boilers/turbogenerators 7%, reactor (KŚT 349) 14%, other technical devices 10% (allocate CAPEX by KŚT group; nuclear island split reactor 14% / BOP 7% / civil 2.5–4.5%). Thin-cap 30% EBITDA (check infrastructure exemption). Loss carry-forward 5 y/50%. Minimum tax 10% applicable from year 4 if profitability <2% and not sheltered by a ≥75% group — include as risk. No PSI/SEZ CIT exemption for generation.
5. **Property tax (gmina revenue):** 2% of the tax value of *budowle* only (post-2025 definitions). Central: 15–30% of overnight CAPEX qualifies as budowle (civil works, cooling, intake, foundations, networks, stacks) → for a 2 bn EUR unit (≈8.7 bn PLN) ≈ **26–52 mln PLN/yr per unit** initially (no depreciation of base for budowle), plus negligible building m² tax; low case 8% of CAPEX (≈14 mln), high 40% (≈70 mln). Benchmark sanity: Kleszczów 144 mln (5 GW + mine), Bełchatów gmina ≈70 mln. **50% of SMR property tax must be passed to bordering gminas (art. 50).**
6. **Local income-tax shares (2025 system):** gmina 7.0% and powiat 2.0%, województwo 0.35% of resident employees' PIT income; gmina 1.6%, powiat 1.7%, województwo 2.3% of the SPV's CIT income (allocated to plant location by employment). Per FTE at 15 k PLN/month gross: ≈10.7 k PLN/yr to gmina. Employer ZUS ≈20.5%; employee ZUS 13.71%; PIT 12%/32% with 30 k tax-free.
7. **Macro:** CPI 2026 3.0–3.4% → 2027 2.7% → long-run 2.5%; NBP ref 3.75% (risk of hikes to 4.25–4.5%); 10-y PLN yield 6.3% (Sept 2026) → long-run risk-free 5.0–5.5% nominal; EUR/PLN 4.36, USD/PLN 3.80; ERP 5.3% (Damodaran); nominal post-tax WACC: merchant nuclear 9–10.5%, CfD-backed 6.5–8% (PEJ Table 1), gas CCGT/OCGT merchant 8–9.5% (judgement).
8. **Gas alternative inputs (Sept 2026):** TGE gas 2027 ≈214–240 PLN/MWh (≈50–55 EUR), spot ≈280–380; EUA 82–84 EUR/t; capacity market 465 PLN/kW/yr (15-y for new gas at 2030 auction, KWD 0.93).

## Key uncertainties / gaps

- **Official long-term Polish price curve (KPEiK 2025 / PSE / URE)** not retrieved — the single largest modelling gap; also no public EY/PwC/Compass Lexecon curve found.
- **OSGE CfD terms** (tenor, indexation, reference price, negative-price treatment) are undisclosed; all design assumptions are inferred from the PEJ precedent. Ministry of Energy has not committed to a scheme or an EC notification timeline.
- The "115–135 EUR/MWh" range in the brief was not found; published OSGE figures are 125–145 EUR/MWh (Oct 2025) and "≈120 EUR/MWh fleet average by 2040".
- PEJ strike-price **real-terms base year** unconfirmed (press: 2036).
- Whether the post-2025 property-tax definitions treat reactor-building internals, containment, and equipment foundations as *budowle* is untested for nuclear; wide range (8–40% of CAPEX).
- Post-2030 capacity mechanism unlegislated; nuclear participation and contract length for a 2035+ COD unknown.
- Minimum-tax exposure of a stand-alone SPV after year 3; thin-cap infrastructure exemption applicability.
- URE regulatory WACC 2026, utility-reported WACCs, Polish project-finance margins/DSCR — not found (search budget).
- Capacity-auction prices for delivery years 2021–2023, 2025, 2026 quoted from memory (need URE confirmation): 240.32 / 198.00 / 202.99 / 172.85 / 400.39.
- Balancing-market reform (June 2024) parameters not verified (PSE pages returned 404).
- Stalowa Wola PSE connection status and site-specific COD not published.
- 2026 negative-hour counts differ by source and by hour vs 15-min counting after 1 Oct 2025.
- Sept 2026 price spike (gas/LNG) may reverse; forward curve snapshot is highly volatile — re-pull TGE data at model freeze.
