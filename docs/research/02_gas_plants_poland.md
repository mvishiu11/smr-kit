### 2.3 Maintenance regime (EOH, overhauls, LTSA)
- Inspection philosophy: combustion inspection → hot-gas-path inspection → major inspection; major inspections in the reviewed LTSA example at **48,000 and 96,000 operating hours**; major maintenance = **50–80 % of lifetime maintenance cost**, of which ≥80 % parts (https://www.power-eng.com/gas/turbines/long-term-service-agreementsweighing-the-risks-and-rewards/ ). Typical OEM intervals for F/H class: CI ~8,000–12,000 EOH, HGPI ~24,000–33,000 EOH, MI ~48,000–66,000 EOH; each start counted as ~10–20 EOH **[PROXY – industry rule of thumb, not fetched]**.
- Polish LTSA values disclosed: Dolna Odra 12-yr service **>1.0 bn PLN** for 1,366 MW → **≈61 PLN/kW-yr**; Rybnik LTSA >0.76 bn PLN (duration not disclosed; if 12 yr → ~72 PLN/kW-yr); Kozienice LTSA **170.09 m USD** for 1,336 MW (≈630 m PLN; if 12 yr → ~39 PLN/kW-yr, excludes hours-based fees) (§1.1 sources).
- PGE OCGT service embedded in 3.5 bn PLN per site (12 yr) — split not disclosed.

### Explicit uncertainties (ranked)
1. **Gas price path 2027–2030**: crisis-driven spot (≈80 EUR/MWh Sep 2026) vs pre-crisis LNG-glut expectations (25–30); no verified Cal-28/29/30 settlements — forward curve above is a proxy. ±50 % on fuel cost.
2. **Capex level and timing**: Polish EPC rose ~50 % in 3 years; turbine slots into 2030 are scarce; 300 MW-class units carry a scale penalty and few 2025–26 European price points; reservation fees and 3–7 yr lead times may make COD before 2031 impossible.
3. **Capacity-market continuation after the 2030 delivery year**: no main auction is scheduled; a redesigned mechanism is expected but not legislated — the 400–465 PLN/kW-yr revenue line could be zero.
4. **EUA trajectory**: 2026 range 70–92; 2030 consensus 126 but range 80–147; Jul-2026 EC review (slower cap cut post-2030) is bearish.
5. **Balancing-service prices**: 2025 aFRR ~180 PLN/MW/h is an early-market anomaly under regulatory scrutiny; expect 50–90 by 2027.
6. **Start-up costs and part-load curves**: only Kumar 2012 (US, 2012 $) and a single academic example were sourced; OEM-specific data for the chosen frame is needed.
7. **Gas transmission tariff**: 2026 exit rates derived from 2025 base + announced % change; the 2027 tariff is announced as "significantly lower" but unquantified.
8. **EC Stalowa Wola operating data** (load factor, starts, availability) not public in sources reached; the BAZTECH operational-experience paper should be obtained.
9. **Licence-fee reform** (×10, uncapped, from 2027) is at consultation stage.
10. **PL/EUR FX** (4.27–4.36 in 2026) and inflation indexation of capacity payments.
